# Construct and source ledger

**Registry:** `EXP-COMP` / `COMP-COMP-POST`  
**Design status:** `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS`  
**Project snapshot:** 2026-08-11

## Construct boundary

Postsecondary composition is situated composing, reading, reasoning, revising, and technology use for college, professional, and community purposes. It includes rhetorical knowledge, critical reading/thinking, composing processes, conventions/language, genre/medium, material conditions, accessibility, and authorship. It is not more high-school essay writing or one universal score scale.
